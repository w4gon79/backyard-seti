Incoherent Stacking

Incoherent stacking combines multiple observation epochs of the same target by aligning and averaging their frequency spectra. This suppresses random noise (which decreases as 1/√N) while preserving narrowband signals that persist across epochs — potentially revealing signals too weak to detect in any single observation.
